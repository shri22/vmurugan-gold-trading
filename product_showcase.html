Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about adopting our platform for your jewellery brand.

Can we launch this with our own Jewellery Showroom name & logo? +
Yes, 100%! The entire system is white-labeled. Your mobile apps on Google Play Store and Apple App Store will have your brand name, logo, custom color palette, store addresses, contact phone numbers, and custom terms & conditions.
How does money reach the jeweller's bank account? +
All customer UPI and online payments are routed directly into your own merchant account with Omniware, Worldline, or Razorpay. Settlements occur automatically in T+1 days directly into your registered current bank account.
Can we create custom scheme types (e.g. 11-month Plus, Flexi Daily)? +
Yes! You have full control over scheme types, durations (6 months, 11 months, 12 months), bonus installments, minimum entry amounts (e.g. ₹10 or ₹500), and whether the scheme accumulates gold grams or cash values.
What if an offline customer pays cash at our showroom counter? +
Our Admin Dashboard includes a dedicated "Adjust Balance" feature. Your counter staff can instantly credit gold/silver grams or cash values for offline payments, with mandatory audit remarks to maintain 100% accurate books.
